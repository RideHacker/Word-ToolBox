About Word ToolBox

Fast, Private & Free Online Text Utilities

Our Mission

Word ToolBox was built to provide writers, developers, students, and content creators with a clean, lightweight, and distraction-free workspace for everyday text manipulation tasks.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Unlike traditional web converters, Word ToolBox operates entirely inside your web browser using modern JavaScript. Your text is never uploaded to remote servers, saved to databases, or shared with third parties.

What We Offer

  • Real-time word, character, sentence, and reading time counters.
  • Instant text casing tools (UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case).
  • Text sorting, line cleaning, and duplicate removal tools.
  • Lorem Ipsum generator and encoding utilities.
